Me

Hi, I'm Raphaël, 47, from Belgium.

Most people call me Raph. I train because it makes everything else in my life better: more energy, a clearer head, and a body that keeps up. This site is where I share what I do and what I learn along the way.

Most of my training is strength work and running, with a bit of cycling when the weather and the legs allow it. I'm not a pro and I don't pretend to be. I just show up, keep it simple and keep it fun.

Consistency beats intensity.
