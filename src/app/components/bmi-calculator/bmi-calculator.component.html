Fill in your height and weight and your BMI shows up here.

BMI is your weight divided by your height squared. It is a quick screen, not a diagnosis. It cannot tell muscle from fat, so strong, muscular people often score "overweight" while being lean and healthy. It also ignores where you carry your weight and your age. If you have a medical condition, ask a doctor or dietitian.
